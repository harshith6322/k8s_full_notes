### 1️⃣ Cluster Architecture, Installation & Configuration

### 2️⃣ Workloads & Scheduling (HIGH WEIGHT)

- StatefulSet

- Jobs & CronJobs

### 3️⃣ Services & Networking (VERY IMPORTANT)

### 4️⃣ Storage

### 5️⃣ Configuration & Secrets

### 6️⃣ Security (IMPORTANT)
