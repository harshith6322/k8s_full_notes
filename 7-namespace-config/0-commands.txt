ns = (namespace)

---get
⚡ kubectl get ns  ==>get all namespaces
⚡ kubectl get all (--all-namespaces or -A)
⚡ kubectl get all -n kube-system   ==> get all things in specific ns
kubectl get po -n kube-system   ==>get all pods in specific ns
kubectl get deploy -n kube-system
kubectl get rs -n kube-system
kubectl get svc -n kube-system
